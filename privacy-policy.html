Stillwater Solitaire Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This policy explains how the Stillwater Solitaire app for iPhone and iPad ("the app," "we," "us") handles information. Stillwater Solitaire is developed by the developer named on its App Store page.

The short version: the app does not collect your personal information, has no accounts, and sends nothing about you to us or to anyone else.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not collect, receive, or have access to any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or other contact details
  • Your location
  • Your contacts, photos, or other content on your device
  • Device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or usage analytics
  • Payment information

The app has no sign-in, no servers of its own, and does not connect to the internet for gameplay.

2. Information stored on your device

To work properly, the app saves a small amount of information on your device only. We never receive a copy of it and cannot see it:

  • Settings: your choices such as card theme, dark mode, whether the timer and score are shown, and board position.
  • Game statistics: games played and won, win percentage, best time, streaks, and total score. These are shown on the Statistics screen.
  • Free-game counter: a single number recording how many of the app's free games you have used (see section 4).

The app does not save the game you are in the middle of. If you close it, the next launch starts a new deal.

If you back up your device, Apple may include this saved information in your backup. Backups are controlled by you and Apple, not by us.

3. Subscriptions and purchases

Stillwater Solitaire offers optional auto-renewing subscriptions to keep playing after the free games. All purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store:

  • Your payment details, Apple ID, and billing information are processed by Apple. We never see them.
  • The app only asks Apple whether a subscription is currently active on your device, so it knows whether to unlock unlimited play.
  • You can restore a previous purchase from the paywall using "Restore Purchases," and you can manage or cancel a subscription at any time in your Apple ID account settings.

Apple's handling of your information is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

4. What stays on your device after you delete the app

Deleting the app removes your settings and game statistics. The free-game counter is the exception: it is stored in your device's secure Keychain, which can survive deleting and reinstalling the app. This is intentional, so that reinstalling does not reset the number of free games. The counter contains only a number and no personal information.

5. Third-party services

The app does not include third-party code, analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind. It is built only with software provided by Apple. We do not use your information to track you across other apps or websites.

If you have chosen to share crash or analytics data with app developers in your iPhone or iPad settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous, aggregated reports about the app. These reports cannot be used to identify you.

6. We do not sell or share your data

Because we do not collect personal information, we have none to sell, rent, or share.

7. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

8. Your choices and rights

Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export, or delete on our end. You can remove your settings and game statistics at any time by deleting the app. If you have a question about your privacy, contact us using the details below.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date above. If we ever change the app in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this policy before the change takes effect.

10. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, email us at adam@arook.net.
