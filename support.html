Frequently asked questions

How do the free games work?
Your first 3 games are free. A game counts once you make your first move in it, so opening the app or dealing a new board doesn't use one up. After your third game, choose a subscription to keep playing.

What subscriptions are available?
Monthly, every 3 months, or yearly. All three unlock unlimited games, and they renew automatically until you cancel. Prices are shown on the subscription screen in the app.

I subscribed but the app still asks me to subscribe.
Open the subscription screen and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Open the Settings app, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, choose Stillwater Solitaire, and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period. Subscriptions are managed by Apple, so we are not able to cancel or refund them from our side. To request a refund, use Apple's Report a Problem page.

How do I undo a move?
Tap the undo arrow in the toolbar, or swipe from right to left across the empty part of the board. Make a decent-sized swipe so accidental touches don't undo anything.

How do I move a card?
Drag a card where you want it, or tap a card to select it and then tap its destination. To deal from the stock, tap it, or double-tap the empty space below the cards.

How do I reach the cards with one hand?
Open Settings (the gear icon), tap Game Board Position, and slide up or down to move the whole board. Double-tap to go back to playing.

How do I change the card theme or use dark mode?
Choose a theme from the Settings screen (the gear icon). Tap the moon icon in the toolbar to switch dark mode on or off.

Does the app work offline?
Yes. You can play without an internet connection. You only need to be online to subscribe or restore a purchase.

Does the app collect my data?
No. Your settings and statistics stay on your device. Read the full Privacy Policy.
